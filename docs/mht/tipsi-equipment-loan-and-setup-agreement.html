2. Equipment (still ours)

We loan you the equipment in Schedule A (the “Equipment”). Title, ownership and all proprietary rights stay with us. You receive a limited, non-exclusive right to use the Equipment at the venue above, only with the Tipsi service, for as long as this agreement is in force.

You must not sell, give away, pledge, hire out, charge, or otherwise dispose of the Equipment. You must not remove Tipsi branding, serial labels, or asset tags. You must not load other payment or ordering software onto the device without our written consent.

3. Setup fee — not the price of the machine

Setup fee €250.00 (two hundred and fifty euros), excluding any VAT if applicable — or other amount written here: €  
What it pays for On-site or remote setup: pairing the terminal, installing/configuring Tipsi Orders, building or loading the menu, QR table stickers or equivalent guest access, a short staff walkthrough, and making the till live. It is a service fee for setting up.
What it does not pay for It is not the purchase price of the Equipment, not a contribution toward ownership, and not a refundable deposit against the hardware. Paying it does not make the machine yours.
When due / refunds Due on the setup date (or on invoice). Once setup is complete, the fee is earned and non-refundable, including if you later stop using Tipsi or return the Equipment.

Software, card processing, Stripe fees, and any Tipsi or Solvio subscription are separate and are not covered by this fee unless we agree that in writing.

4. Care, loss, and replacement

  1. You will keep the Equipment in your possession, at the venue, in reasonable working condition, and tell us promptly if it is lost, stolen, or damaged beyond ordinary wear.
  2. If the Equipment fails in normal use, we will repair or swap it at our cost where the fault is not caused by misuse, liquid damage, or unauthorised alteration.
  3. If it is lost, stolen, not returned, or damaged by misuse, you will pay the then-current replacement amount. Today that is €150.00 per terminal (or the amount we invoice to actually replace it, if higher).
  4. Risk of loss passes to you on handover. Insurance of the venue’s contents is your responsibility.

5. Term and return

This loan starts on handover and continues until either party ends it on 14 days’ written notice (email is enough), or immediately if you stop using Tipsi, become insolvent, or materially breach this agreement.

When it ends, you will return the Equipment to us (or hold it for collection) within 7 days, with chargers and accessories listed in Schedule A, in the condition you received it apart from fair wear. If you do not, clause 4.3 applies as if the Equipment were not returned.

Returning the machine does not entitle you to a refund of the setup fee.

6. Solvio Show Ops (separate)

This agreement is only about Tipsi hardware and Tipsi setup. It does not sell, licence, or price the Solvio Show Ops desk. Any Show Ops fees are a separate arrangement.

7. General

This is the whole agreement on the Equipment and the setup fee. Changes must be in writing (email is enough). English law applies. The courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction, without limiting any mandatory rights you have where the venue is in Spain.
