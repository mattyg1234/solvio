# Your partner logins, ticket photos and Shows setup

Joel, this is the follow-up to the options you said were confusing. It covers the partner and Shows changes being released on 5 September. It does not change the agreed pricing sheet.

Your partner administrator has a crown beside their role. You or Ruth choose who gets that access in Settings. They can invite their own sellers by email, remove ordinary seller logins and compare sales across their organisation. Sellers see only their own bookings and figures. The results show booking value and passengers over your chosen dates, with pounds and euros kept separate; they do not claim that booked sales are cash received.

The invitation is a one-use sign-in link, including for a brand-new account. After signing in, the seller can set a password or go straight to bookings. Test mode only permits approved email addresses. A blocked or failed invitation now says it was not sent, so you can retry it after the address is cleared.

After making a booking, the seller can attach a photo of the tickets handed to the customer. They can return to their own booking to view or replace its photo. Those original images are available to attach to the partner invoice. Photos remain private; sellers cannot browse another seller's evidence. Each booking currently holds one selected photo, so several tickets should be captured together in that image. The upload limit is 3 MB.

Your Shows page now separates the physical show from the ticket options. Set its island, run days, start time, capacity and transport once. Open the show to edit those details or its prices. The optional nett section explains when the fallback applies instead of leaving it mixed into the main prices.

Under that show you can add named ticket types or complete packages, such as Standard, Gold, Show only or Gold + meal, and enter their own adult, child and infant prices. You choose the names, inclusions and prices. These are examples, not extra charges I have added. All types share the same show capacity, so adding another type does not create extra seats. You can also create optional extras with your own names and prices, charged once per booking, per guest (including infants), or by a quantity the seller chooses. Choose whether each extra earns partner commission. The booking, guest ticket and invoice use the agreed extra prices saved with the booking.

For transport prices, you can set an explicit price with the bus and another without it. If you leave the without-transport price blank, the main price is used for the ticket and your configured supplement is added for an adult or child travelling by bus. That rule applies separately to each age band. UK Tour prices are shown in pounds; your island prices are in euros.

Existing bookings retain their agreed prices and selected ticket type. Renaming or repricing a type does not rewrite that history. Switching off transport later also leaves an existing bus booking intact when you only change a guest detail. Archiving a type stops new sales while keeping its past bookings.

The explicit repricing button is different: it uses the saved show/type and current partner rates to change existing, uncancelled bookings that have not been invoiced, including past and future dates. It is not a scheduled future price change. Save the prices first and only use that button when you intend to change those bookings.

You can limit each user's access to particular islands as well as choosing their role. A restricted administrator can manage their permitted islands but cannot expand their own access. Shared settings and changes to access remain with you or an administrator who has unrestricted access.

When you type a hotel or pick-up name, the search also checks the linked hotel or stop. Your hotel keeps its accommodation details; your bus stop supplies the collection instructions, map/photo link and guide notes used by the bus list.

On the bus board you can drag stops into order, or use the move buttons, and save that order for the night. The bus list uses that same saved order. It has print, PDF download and email controls, and asks you to save outstanding changes before sending or exporting. The printed browser list can show pick-up photos; the PDF includes clickable photo and map links. Email still follows your test-mode delivery restrictions.

Your booking page shows who created it and the date and time. From this update onward, creation, edits, cancellations, ticket-photo changes and payment changes are recorded automatically, with the person, time and before/after values. If recording an edit fails, the edit itself is rolled back. Earlier history remains visible, but changes that were never recorded cannot be reconstructed; the page explains that limitation.
