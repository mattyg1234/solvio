Hire a boat

We have a range of boats available to hire to suit most needs and budgets. We provide buoyancy aids on all craft (kayak, paddleboard, rowing boat and Canadian canoe) — essential for children 12 and under. We cannot guarantee you will stay dry, so bring a towel and change of clothes. Please read the terms and conditions of hire.

Skippered narrowboat

Cruise on Somersun. Morning, afternoon and evening cruises with SBC staff skippering — up to twelve passengers. Help with locks, mooring and the swing bridge as much or as little as you like. Private hire for birthdays and celebrations. Food options after booking (min 48hrs): fish & chips, cream teas, afternoon teas, breakfast rolls — or bar drinks on arrival.

£180.00 / 2.5 hours

Self drive narrowboat

Somerdays — self-drive day bookings, max 8 people. Picnics welcome or kitchen pre-order (48hrs). Hire 09:30–16:30 including 30 mins training — arrive promptly. £100 deposit on arrival, refunded after post-hire check. Trip towards Taunton; optional stops at The Bathpool Inn or The Merry Monk.

£195.00 / 7 hours

Wheelchair boat

Specially adapted — 4 wheelchair users and 4 carers, or max 10 passengers (1000kg). Skipper included; unique helm for those with upper mobility. No shelter; umbrellas provided. Commercial use for bridge inspection — contact us.

£60.00 / 1 hour

Canadian canoe

Suitable for 2 people. Wide open frame; single-bladed paddles. Max passenger weight 222kg.

£15.00 / 1 hour

Stand up paddleboards (SUPs)

Fun for all ages and skill levels — experienced or not. Max 180kg per board.

£12.50 / 1 hour

Big Billie paddle board

Giant SUP — 18ft × 5ft, up to 8 people. Max weight 500kg.

£50.00 / 1 hour

Kayak single

Stable and comfortable single seater — up to 150kg.

£12.50 / 1 hour

Kayak twin

Two adults or two adults and a toddler — max load 280kg.

£15.00 / 1 hour

Rafted canoes

Two canoes joined catamaran-style — almost impossible to capsize. Great for families. Up to 6 people, max 400kg.

£20.00 / 1 hour
